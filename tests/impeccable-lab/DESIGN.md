# Impeccable lab visual contract

## Purpose

An isolated verification surface, not a product design proposal.

## Fixed version

- restrained editorial character;
- Georgia display/body typography with native serif fallback;
- navy, slate, rust, white and cool-gray palette;
- one flat bordered content surface, no nested cards;
- clear h1 → h2 hierarchy;
- body measure at or below 68ch;
- minimum 44px control height and visible keyboard focus;
- deliberate single-column mobile composition at 390×844;
- no animation, gradients, remote assets, or horizontal overflow.

The fixture may change only to resolve verified defects or improve the test's ability to measure the declared criteria.
