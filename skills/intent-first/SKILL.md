# Intent First

Before implementation, establish:
- desired user/business outcome;
- what must visibly/functionally change;
- constraints;
- approved references;
- what is explicitly out of scope;
- what must not regress.

For small tasks, do this mentally and proceed.
For large tasks, capture the result in PROJECT.md before or alongside implementation.

Do not force the user to specify technical architecture when the outcome is clear. Choose the best technical path yourself.
